Isolated - Chapter 1

Falling. Everything, slipping away from my fingertips. Sunlight. The faint rays of sun being the last thing I saw before my world turned dark. \n Rays of sunlight. Shining specks of dust gently floating in the soft rays of the morning sun. A seemingly ordinary morning, yet it turned out to be a day I'll never forget. \n I wake up to the warm rays of sunlight and stare at the ceiling, waiting for me eyes to focus. Gathering my thoughts, I yawn and stretch before hopping out of the hammock I had made with fabric from tents in camspites which were long abandoned. I glance out of the window, and gaze upon the old cement structure that I lived in.
